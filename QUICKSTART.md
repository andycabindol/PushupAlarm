# Quick Start Guide - Pushup Alarm

## Get It Running in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Transfer to Your Mac
Copy the entire project folder to your Mac.

### Step 2: Open in Xcode
Double-click `PushupAlarm.xcodeproj`

### Step 3: Sign the App
1. Click on "PushupAlarm" in the left sidebar (blue icon)
2. Under "Signing & Capabilities" tab
3. Select your Team (sign in with Apple ID if needed)

### Step 4: Connect iPhone
1. Plug in your iPhone via USB
2. Select it from the device dropdown (top of Xcode)

### Step 5: Run It!
Click the ▶️ button (or press Cmd+R)

**First Run**: Go to Settings > General > Device Management > Trust your developer certificate

---

## Quick Test

1. Open the app
2. Tap "Test Pushup Detection" at the bottom
3. Place the phone on the floor so your head and arms are in frame
4. Do some pushups and watch it count!

---

## Set Your First Alarm

1. Pick a time (try 1-2 minutes from now for testing)
2. Tap "Set Alarm"
3. Lock your phone
4. Wait for the alarm...
5. Do 10 pushups to dismiss it!

---

## Troubleshooting

**Not counting pushups?**
- Keep your head and arms in frame (legs can be out of view)
- Face the front camera with good lighting
- Lower until elbows bend, then push all the way up
- Slow down a bit so each position can register

**Camera permission denied?**
Settings > Privacy > Camera > PushupAlarm > Enable

**Alarm not firing?**
Settings > Notifications > PushupAlarm > Allow Notifications
